1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you play c3F5 (the “Game”). By installing and using the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

The Game contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics services. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Multiplayer and Photon

To provide online multiplayer, the Game uses Photon Fusion 2, a service provided by Exit Games GmbH. When you join an online session, the following data may be processed:

  • your device’s IP address;
  • a player identifier assigned by Photon (Photon User ID);
  • your nickname, if the Game lets you set one;
  • technical connection data, such as region, ping, game version, and platform;
  • in-session gameplay data (for example, positions, player actions, and match state) that is shared with other players in the same session.

This data is used only to make multiplayer work. It is processed by Photon servers in accordance with Photon’s privacy policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

4. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to connect players and operate the online mode. We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, other than the Photon service required to run multiplayer.

5. Data Storage

We do not maintain our own user database. Connection data is processed by Photon for as long as necessary to operate the service and under Photon’s retention rules. Local Game data (settings, progress) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

6. Nicknames and Text Chat

Your nickname and the text messages you send in the in-game chat are visible to other players in your session. Chat messages are transmitted through Photon servers only to deliver them to other players. We do not store chat messages on our own servers or use them for any other purpose.

Please do not share personal information in your nickname or in chat, since other players can see it. If you encounter abusive or inappropriate messages, contact us at whitekittengames@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will help with its deletion.

8. Your Rights

You may request information about data related to you, or its deletion, by contacting email@example.com. Because we do not store user data on our own servers, some requests may need to be directed to Photon (Exit Games GmbH).

9. Security

Connections to Photon servers use the security measures provided by the service. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this link, and the effective date above will be updated accordingly.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at whitekittengames@gmail.com.
